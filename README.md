# 🌍 Air Quality Index (AQI) Analysis in Delhi

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an in-depth analysis of the **Air Quality Index (AQI) in Delhi** using data analysis and visualization techniques. The objective is to understand the city's air pollution trends by examining major pollutants, seasonal variations, and geographical influences that affect air quality. The findings aim to support environmental awareness and data-driven decision-making for improving public health.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the overall Air Quality Index (AQI) in Delhi.
* Identify the major pollutants contributing to poor air quality.
* Study seasonal variations in AQI levels.
* Examine the impact of geographical and environmental factors on pollution.
* Generate meaningful insights through statistical analysis and visualizations.

## ❓ Research Questions

* Which pollutants contribute the most to Delhi's AQI?
* How does AQI vary across different seasons?
* What trends can be observed in air pollution over time?
* How do geographical and environmental conditions influence AQI?
* What recommendations can help improve air quality in Delhi?
